How It Works

Show a tray with 6–8 small items for 30 seconds, cover it, and ask learners to name as many as they can.

Discuss how a "plan" helps remember details!

Online Version

Perfect for Zoom or learning platforms like SHANA. Use digital slides with bright, fun images for an engaging virtual experience.

How to Set It Up

Follow these simple steps for a successful game

1
Prepare a digital tray

Use a Google Slide, PowerPoint, or Canva slide with 6–8 fun, bright images (e.g., pencil, book, ruler, balloon, hat, flower). Label it "Look closely!"

2
Show the screen

Share your screen for 30 seconds while learners observe. Tell them: "Try to remember everything you see!"

3
Hide one item

Switch to a slide with one object removed. Build suspense and ask: "What's missing?"

4
Learner response

Learners respond verbally, in chat, or by raising hands. Celebrate correct answers & discuss memory strategies.
