End of Module Project

"I Think I Can" Journey

Building confidence through the story of The Little Engine That Could

1

Story Recap

Understanding the foundation of our confidence journey

Learners watch the story or trainer retells it (shortened version).

Key message: The little engine was small but believed in herself and kept trying until she succeeded.
2

Learners' Reflection

Connect the story to your own confidence journey

Prompt learners to reflect on their personal experiences:

“What is one thing you thought was hard but you tried anyway?”
“What’s something you want to keep saying ‘I think I can’ about?”
3

Creative Project Options

Build your confidence track

🚂 Confidence Track

Learners build a simple "train track" (paper or digital slides).

Each stop on the track = one confidence skill they learned:

  • Believing in myself
  • Speaking up
  • Celebrating my wins
  • Setting a goal
4

Presentation

Share your journey and celebrate progress

Learners share their project with the class (or upload on the platform).

Trainers highlight progress: “See, just like the Little Engine, you believed in yourself and kept going.”

Key Takeaways

💪 Confidence

"I believe I can."

🎯 Planning

"I can break big goals into small steps."

Remember: Just like the Little Engine, you can achieve anything with confidence and perseverance.
