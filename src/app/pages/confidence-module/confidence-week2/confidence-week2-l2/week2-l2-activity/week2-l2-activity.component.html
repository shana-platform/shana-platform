In this TEDxYouth talk, Nelisiwe Sibanda shares how being different is not something to hide but something to celebrate. She explains that our uniqueness is what makes us strong and helps us stand out in the world.

⭐ Key Lesson: You don’t need to be like everyone else to be special. What makes you you , your personality, your kindness, and your choices is what truly matters!
