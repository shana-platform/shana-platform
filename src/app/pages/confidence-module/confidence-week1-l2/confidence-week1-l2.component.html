📖 Stand Tall, Molly Lou Melon

by Patty Lovell

Story Summary

For learners

Molly Lou Melon is small and different, and sometimes people make fun of her. But she remembers her grandmother’s advice to stand tall, smile big, and believe in herself. Because of that, she turns her differences into her strengths.

Try This!

Draw or write about something that makes you unique. How can you use it as a strength?
