Kojo's Adventure

Kojo’s alarm rang, but he turned over and fell back asleep. When he finally woke up, the sun was already high. “Ahh! I’m late!” Kojo said, jumping out of bed. He searched under the bed, behind the door, and inside his bag — but he couldn’t find his socks. His heart beat faster. His hands felt tight. Kojo felt upset and frustrated. At breakfast, he barely touched his food. On the way to school, his mind kept saying, “This day is already bad.” In class, Kojo opened his pencil case and realised he had also forgotten his crayons. His shoulders dropped. Ama, who sat next to him, noticed his quiet face. “Kojo, you can use mine,” she said, sliding her crayons across the table. Kojo looked up. He smiled a small smile at first… then a bigger one. “Thank you,” he said. As he started colouring, his chest felt lighter. The bad morning didn’t feel so heavy anymore. Kojo learned something important that day: Feelings can change — and a small act of kindness can make a big difference.
