Yaw's Adventure

Yaw was sitting on the classroom floor, carefully building a tall tower with his blocks. He worked slowly, placing each block on top of the other. He wanted his tower to be the tallest one in the room. Suddenly, the tower wobbled and crashed to the floor. Bang! The blocks scattered everywhere. Some children nearby laughed when they saw the fallen tower. Yaw’s face grew hot, and his eyes filled with tears. He felt sad and a little embarrassed. He wanted to stop playing and move away. Efua, his friend, noticed Yaw’s quiet face and the blocks on the floor. She walked over and sat beside him. In a kind voice, she said, “It’s okay, Yaw. Sometimes things fall down. Let’s build it again together.” Yaw looked at Efua. Her smile made him feel less alone. He wiped his eyes and nodded. Together, they picked up the blocks and started again. This time, Yaw felt calmer and more confident. Even though the tower was not perfect, Yaw felt better because he had a friend who understood his feelings. Yaw learned that when someone is kind and supportive, it can help hurt feelings feel lighter.
