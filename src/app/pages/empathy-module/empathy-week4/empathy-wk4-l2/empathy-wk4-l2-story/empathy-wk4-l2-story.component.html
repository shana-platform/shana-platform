Zainab's Adventure

After school, dark clouds filled the sky, and soon rain began to fall. The children hurried out of the school gate, some running and some laughing as the rain splashed on the ground. Zainab opened her umbrella and held it over her head. Then she noticed her friend Nii standing nearby. His schoolbag was getting wet, and he had no umbrella to cover himself. He looked worried as the rain grew heavier. Zainab paused for a moment. She looked at her umbrella, then at Nii. Without hesitation, she moved closer and said, “You can share my umbrella.” Nii’s face lit up. “Thank you, Zainab,” he said with a big smile. They walked home together, holding the umbrella tightly between them. Even though their shoes and uniforms became a little wet, they laughed as they stepped over puddles and watched the rain fall around them. When they finally reached home, Zainab felt warm inside. She knew she had helped her friend. Sharing her umbrella made the walk happier for both of them. Zainab learned that helping others, even in small ways, can turn a rainy day into a joyful one.
