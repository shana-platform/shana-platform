Sena's Adventure

Sena borrowed her cousin’s storybook. She kept it safe… until juice spilled on the table. A drop splashed onto the cover. Sena felt scared, but she wiped it gently, dried it, and told her cousin the truth. Her cousin said, “Thank you for taking care of my things and being honest.” Respect is shown in how we treat belongings — ours and others’.
